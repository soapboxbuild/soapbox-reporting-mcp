Impact on Asset Value at Exit — Value-Creation Waterfall

Each plan bridges from incremental capital expense to net value creation at exit: incentives, capitalized owner-share utility savings and ancillary revenue, and the capitalized value of BPS fine avoidance. All recurring NOI changes — utility savings, ancillary revenue, and avoided BPS fines — are capitalized at the exit cap rate (÷ exit cap) on the exit-year run-rate a buyer capitalizes at sale.

Like-for-like is the replacement cost that would be spent anyway; only the incremental cost over it drives value. Net Total Cost = incremental − incentives. Y1 Return on Cost = (landlord utility savings + ancillary revenue) ÷ net total cost.
